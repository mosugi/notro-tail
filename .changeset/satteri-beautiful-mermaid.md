Initial release. Successor of the discontinued `rehype-beautiful-mermaid`: the same build-time Mermaid-to-SVG rendering (via `beautiful-mermaid`), rebuilt as a Sätteri hast plugin for Astro 7's Rust-based Markdown/MDX processor. Export changed from `rehypeMermaid` (rehype plugin) to `satteriMermaid(options)` (plugin factory); the `theme` and `className` options are unchanged.
